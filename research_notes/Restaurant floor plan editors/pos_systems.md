# How restaurant POS systems build floor plans and connect them to seat-level ordering and the kitchen (state as of Nov 2026)

*Labels used below: **(snippet)** means the fact comes from search-indexed text of the cited page because the page itself could not be loaded (403/503/blank render). **(marketing)** means a vendor sales claim. **(community, undated)** means a vendor-forum post with no visible date. **(third-party review)** and **(competitor blog)** mark non-vendor sources. ## 1. Floor-plan editors: tools, objects, device, polish

### Takeaway
The market splits into two kinds of editor. Back-office web editors publish changes to devices: Toast Web, Square Dashboard, Lightspeed K-Series Back Office, Revel Management Console and Oracle Simphony EMC. On-device editors run on the POS itself: TouchBistro on iPad, Aloha Cloud, the Clover Dining app and Lightspeed O-Series. Every editor documented is a simple 2D canvas of tables (shape, size, label, capacity) plus a few barriers, labels and decorative objects. None of the documented editors places individual seats around a table. Most also require an explicit publish or reload step before devices pick up a change.

### Cited Findings

#### Toast (web back office only)
- Layouts are built only in Toast Web, under Front of House > Service areas & tables > Service areas > "+ Add Service Area" > "Edit Tables". The POS does not edit layouts. It only displays the published layout. — [Toast: Create Service Areas and Table Setup (updated Oct 5, 2026)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430)
- The editor has a "Tables" palette and a "Barriers & Labels" palette, with drag-and-drop onto a layout area. Selecting a table opens its settings at the bottom of the page. Editable properties are table number, size, width, height and revenue center. Toast offers "a few table shapes" but the article does not name them. — [Toast: Create Service Areas](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430)
- Labels hold free text such as "Sidewalk" and can be resized. Barriers can surround tables, but decorative borders "can cause problems with accessing or editing tables". A small table may hide its timer, so Toast advises enlarging it. — [Toast: Create Service Areas](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430)
- Service-area settings are name, primary flag and revenue center. A service area cannot be hidden or archived, only deleted, and "Undo Remove" works until you save. Changes need Save, then Publish. If a layout does not reach the POS, staff "resync all data". Editing requires permission "6.2 Kitchen/Dining Room Setup". — [Toast: Create Service Areas](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430)
- Layouts are designed for the landscape terminal screen. Portrait handhelds rotate the layout to fit, which Toast calls expected behavior. — [Toast: Create Service Areas](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430)
- Service Areas are a different product from "Toast Tables", Toast's host and reservations add-on. — [Toast: Create Service Areas (snippet)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430)
- API data model: the `Table` object has only `guid`, `entityType`, `name`, `serviceArea` and `revenueCenter`. It has no coordinates, shape, seat count or capacity. — [Toast API: Table schema](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/configuration/tag/Data-definitions/schema/Table). `ServiceArea` has `guid`, `entityType`, `name`, `serviceAreaType` and a `revenueCenter` reference. — [Toast API: ServiceArea schema (snippet)](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/configuration/tag/Data-definitions/schema/ServiceArea)
- Integrators import Toast service areas as their areas. If Toast returns no service areas, nothing is imported. — [storekit Toast dine-in guide (snippet)](https://storekit.com/docs/guides/integrations/pos/toast/dine-in)

#### Square for Restaurants (web Dashboard)
- Floor plans are created in Square Dashboard > Settings > Restaurant settings > Floor plans > "Create a floor plan", with a name and a location. A location can have several floor plans, for example one for lunch and one for dinner. — [Square: Building your floor plan](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6427)
- Sections group tables by area (dining room, bar, patio). Each section takes a name, custom or automatic table names, an optional label and a number of tables. — [Square: Building your floor plan](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6427); [Square: Create a floor plan section (snippet)](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8145-create-a-floor-plan-section-with-square-for-restaurants)
- Clicking a table lets you move it or change its size and shape. Tables can be dragged or moved with the arrow keys, and drawing a box selects several at once. An older version of the article described clicking "a numbered square" to add a table. — [Square: Building your floor plan (IE version, snippet)](https://squareup.com/help/ie/article/6427)
- The trash icon removes a table from the plan without deleting its data. Floor plans require Square for Restaurants Plus or Premium, or Square Plus/Premium with advanced restaurant capabilities, plus full service or bar mode. — [Square: Building your floor plan](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6427)
- One community answer says a section holds at most 100 tables. This may be dated. — [Square Seller Community (community)](https://www.sellercommunity.com/t5/Questions-How-To/Restaurants-Can-a-Table-Section-in-my-floor-plan-have-unlimited/m-p/99227)

#### Lightspeed Restaurant K-Series (web Back Office)
- Floor plans are added in Back Office > Business > Floor plans > Add, and each name must be unique. The Settings tab holds "Order profile", "Prompt cover count", "Background image" and "Printing profiles". Background images must be JPG or PNG under 2 MB, with an ideal 15:8 ratio. Images larger than 900×480 are rescaled. — [Lightspeed K: Adding floor plans and tables](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/1260804656709)
- Tables: up to 150 per floor plan. Each table has a "Number of covers" (seats), a "Table type" of round or square, and a "Table name" that must be unique across all floor plans, up to 4 letters or numbers. Prefixes such as B for bar and D for dining room group tables into sections. You drag tables to position them and drag their edges to resize. — [Lightspeed K: Adding floor plans and tables](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/1260804656709)
- Changes reach the POS only after Settings > Control center > "Reload" on the POS. Disabling a table hides it on the POS but keeps its reporting. Deleting a floor plan "cannot be undone". — [Lightspeed K: Editing floor plans and tables](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/1260804656729); [Lightspeed K: Adding floor plans](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/1260804656709)
- Advanced table fields: a "Reference number" ties an RFID/NFC tag to Order Anywhere, and a numeric "Table ID" serves integrations that cannot handle alphanumeric names. — [Lightspeed K: Adding floor plans](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/1260804656709)
- The API lists "Get All Floorplans", "Get Floorplan Tables", "Get All Open Checks", "Get Open Check by Table" and "Platform course settings definition". No seat or table-status fields are visible on the index page. — [Lightspeed K-Series API docs](https://api-docs.lsk.lightspeed.app/). Access is reportedly gated to approved partners. — [Supergood API report (snippet)](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/lightspeed)
- An integrator's sync matches tables by their Lightspeed table number. Renumbering a table makes the sync treat it as deleted, so it is removed and recreated. — [storekit Lightspeed dine-in guide (snippet)](https://storekit.com/docs/guides/integrations/pos/lightspeed/dine-in)
- Lightspeed O-Series, a different product line, edits on the POS. The editor adds, moves and edits tables, chairs, flooring and sections after "Tables" is enabled in Back Office. — [Lightspeed O-Series help (snippet)](https://o-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/31329411093659)

#### Oracle MICROS Simphony (EMC enterprise config → workstation pages)

### Inferences
- All documented editors are furniture-placement tools: a table is a shape, a size, a label and a capacity number. Overcooked's model goes further than any of them: a grid with walls and doors, a chosen side for each seat, and a seat service order.
- Back-office vendors put every change behind an explicit step: Toast's "Save → Publish", Lightspeed's POS "Reload" and Aloha's "Apply Changes". This keeps half-finished edits away from live service. Overcooked edits on tablets, so a draft-then-publish step (with versions) would give the same protection.
- Several vendors keep a table's identity separate from where it sits on a plan. Simphony's Tables module is separate from page placement and must match by "Table #". Lightspeed table names are unique across all floor plans. Toast uses a `guid` that is separate from `name`. This matches Overcooked's restaurant-wide tables. The Lightspeed integration case, where renumbering caused a delete and recreate, shows why display numbers should not double as identifiers.
- Multiple layouts per area have precedents. Square allows lunch and dinner floor plans per location. Simphony activates "section layouts", and merges and overrides end when a layout is deactivated. Overcooked needs a similar rule for what happens to live state when the active layout changes.

### Gaps
- Toast's shape names, rotation and background-image support are not documented in the article. Square's shapes and rotation are not documented either.
- SpotOn's editor location, Clover's official setup docs and Revel's full article text could not be retrieved. Revel's Salesforce site renders blank. TouchBistro's and Upserve's help sites returned 503 ([Upserve floorplan article](https://help.upserve.com/articles/en_US/Breadcrumb/Floorplan-Management-Add-and-Change-Tables)).
- No primary documentation was found for Tevalis or Lavu floor editors.
- No source showed undo/redo history, snapping rules, or tablet-versus-desktop parity for any editor.

## 2. Seat representation: numbering, positions, shared items, display

### Takeaway
Seats are almost always a number on each order line plus a cover or capacity count on the table, not chair positions drawn on the plan. Shared items are handled in three ways: Toast uses a "Share" bucket (`seatNumber` 0), Square lets one item belong to several seats, and Lightspeed has a fully shared ordering mode. "Pivot seating" appears in Aloha settings only as a rule that forces numbered seats. Kitchen output ranges from seat numbers beside items, as in Toast and Square, to tickets grouped by seat, as in Simphony's "Chit w/ Seat Separator".

### Cited Findings
- **Toast settings:** seat numbers can be Required, Optional or Off, set separately for Quick Order and Table Service. Toast recommends turning on Party Size, which creates the seat buttons. — [Toast: Enable seat numbers and party sizes (updated Dec 31, 2025; snippet)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Seat-Numbers-Enabling-and-Configuring-1493086465089)
- **Toast Order by Seat** is on by default. With Required, the check shows numbered seat headers from the guest count. With Optional, a "Share" header and an add-seat button appear. Adding seats does not change the guest count, which supports non-consecutive seats "such as 1, 2, and 4". Prev. seat and Next seat buttons move between seats. Items can be moved to another seat even after sending. — [Toast: Use Order by Seat (updated Sep 18, 2026)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Order-by-Seat)
- **Toast kitchen side:** "Enabling seat numbers does not change how seats appear in the kitchen." If a seat number changes after sending, a change notice prints or shows on the KDS, depending on Kitchen > "Print Ticket Changes". The Share header cannot be turned off. With Order by Seat off, seats become a modifier group shown under each item, and the check groups by course. — [Toast: Use Order by Seat](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Order-by-Seat); [Toast: Remove the Share Section (snippet)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Can-I-remove-the-share-section-from-orders-on-my-POS)
- **Toast one-seat-per-item limit:** "the POS currently allows assigning a menu item to only one seat position. It does not support marking two or more seat positions for one plate." The workaround is to split the item before sending. — [Toast Community "Seat positions" (community manager reply; snippet, page 403)](https://community.toasttab.com/t5/restaurant-operations/seat-positions/m-p/13485)
- **Toast API:** `Selection.seatNumber` takes a positive integer for the seat, `0` for "shared by multiple guests" and `-1` when "the restaurant employee did not select a seat". It is response-only. — [Toast API: Selection schema](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/orders/tag/Data-definitions/schema/Selection/)
- **Toast ticket sorting:** "Item Sorting Priority" can be "By seat number", "By sort order" or input order. "By seat number" requires the seat-number UI options and Party size set to "Always ask before creating a new order". — [Toast Kitchen settings reference](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminKitchenDiningRoomReference.html). When sort order is used, items sort by sort order first and seat second, and seat numbers "show on the ticket or screen next to the item". — [Toast: Manage item sorting priority (snippet)](https://intercom.help/toast-central/en/articles/9320673-manage-item-sorting-priority-for-kitchen-tickets). Grid-view KDS tickets now show seat numbers when available. — [Toast release announcement (snippet; one of two announcements returned)](https://updates.toasttab.com/announcements/ann_rEx91Sqn2BJ8h)
- **Toast marketing:** seat-level assignment is pitched for allergies and dietary preferences in fine dining. — [Toast fine dining page (marketing, snippet)](https://pos.toasttab.com/ie/restaurant-pos/fine-dining)
- **Square:** seat tracking is turned on per device under More > Settings > Checkout > Seating > "Track Seating", with "Track cover count with optional seat positions" or "…with required seat positions". Staff use "Add Seat", "Move to Seat", "Move All Items to Seat", "Create new check" (a check from one seat), "Transfer to Check" (the seat becomes a new seat on another check) and "Sort cart > By Seat". **Items assigned to multiple seats are split automatically** when the check is split. Items can be sorted by seat on printed kitchen tickets and the KDS. — [Square: Manage seats in your restaurant](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8583-manage-seats-in-your-restaurant)
- **Square split by seat:** Actions > Split > "Split by seat" creates a check for each seat that has items. — [Square: Split a payment and check (snippet)](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8165-split-a-payment-and-check-with-square-for-restaurants). An older community answer said splitting whole seats was not possible. The current help article supersedes it. — [Square community (community, date unclear; snippet)](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Archived-Discussions-Read-Only/How-to-split-checks-based-on-seats/m-p/104210)
- **Square conflict:** older community threads asked for seat information on the KDS, and Square staff gave "no ETA". — [Square community "Seating on KDS" (community, undated)](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Feature-Requests/Seating-on-KDS/idi-p/344634). The current help article says items can be sorted by seat on the KDS. — [Square: Manage seats](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8583-manage-seats-in-your-restaurant)
- **Lightspeed K:** "Number of covers" per table drives split-by-seat billing and reporting. "Prompt cover count" asks for occupied seats when a table is opened. — [Lightspeed K: Adding floor plans](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/1260804656709). - **TouchBistro:** seats are color-coded on the order screen. Item properties print on kitchen tickets with the seat number, but "these properties will not get sent to the KDS". — [TouchBistro help: Taking orders (snippet; attribution approximate)](https://cdn.touchbistro.com/help/articles/taking-orders). A "Seat Prompt" asks which seat an added item belongs to. — [Revel: Table Service Settings (snippet)](https://support.revelsystems.com/hc/en-us/articles/204949855-Table-Service-Settings); [Revel TSR POS Guide (snippet)](https://support.revelsystems.com/hc/en-us/articles/204576105-Table-Service-Restaurant-TSR-Point-of-Sale-Guide)

### Inferences
- No documented POS links seat numbers to chairs drawn on the floor plan. Aloha comes closest with a per-table seat count of 1–20, and TouchBistro lets you place chairs and stools as layout objects, but no source says those objects map to seat numbers. Overcooked's per-side seat placement with a service order would be a real differentiator.
- Toast's non-consecutive seats (1, 2, 4) and Aloha's "Pivot seating transfer merges seats" both show that seat numbers carry positional meaning and can collide when tables merge. Overcooked keeps seat numbers stable across layouts, so it needs a clear rule for what a merge does to seat identity.
- Shared items: Toast's single-seat limit and `seatNumber 0` bucket fit table-shared plates badly when only some seats share them, for example a bottle of wine for seats 1–3. Square's multi-seat assignment with automatic splitting fits Overcooked's per-seat drinks and pairings better, alongside an explicit table-level bucket.
- For kitchen output, Simphony's "Chit w/ Seat Separator" is the closest precedent for Overcooked's per-table tickets with seat-specific restrictions. Toast and Square put seat numbers beside items. TouchBistro shows that seat properties can fail to reach the KDS at all, a failure mode to avoid.

### Gaps
- No vendor document found defines a physical "pivot point" convention (seat 1 relative to the door or kitchen) or shows one on the map.
- Seat numbers on guest receipts are not documented for Toast, Square or Lightspeed K.
- TouchBistro's shared-seat and combine-seats behavior could not be retrieved.
- Square's public API was not checked for seat or floor-plan fields.

## 3. Coursing and firing, and how the KDS shows seats and courses

### Takeaway
There are three firing models. Servers fire courses by hand with Hold and Send. Expediters fire them. Or the system fires them automatically, either in sequence after the previous course is done or on a timer. Toast has the richest documented configuration. The floor map usually shows only table-level held, sent or done status, not which course a table is on. Simphony's enhanced-table overlays and Lightspeed K's "coursing status" table view are the exceptions. KDS support varies: Square can hide held courses, Toast can show previous-course status on tickets, and TouchBistro does not support holds with its KDS.

### Cited Findings
- **Toast setup:** courses are created under Kitchen > Pacing > Courses and assigned to menu groups. Course pacing is turned on under Kitchen > Pacing > Meal pacing. "Course Pacing and Server Item Firing cannot be enabled at the same time." Coursing works only for in-store orders, not online ordering or integrations. Course assignment can be Required (sending is blocked until every item has a course), Optional (a warning appears) or Off. With "Send courses individually", servers use Send, Hold and Stay on the POS. With "Send all courses to kitchen immediately", Toast Web rules decide what fires. — [Toast: Manage Course Firing Options (updated Sep 15, 2026)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Course-Firing-Options)
- **Toast kitchen settings:** "Firing Courses" can be "Server fires courses individually", "Automate course firing" or "Expediter fires courses individually". "Automated Firing" (KDS only) fires the next course after the previous one is fulfilled by all prep stations or the expediter, or on a timed "Course Firing Schedule" with an "Optional delay between courses". "Expediter Override" controls whether expo can fire held courses early. "Item Fire by Prep Time" schedules items to finish together. "Held Tickets" can show at both expo and prep stations or at expo only, and printed tickets always show held items. "Previous Course Status" shows earlier courses' status on kitchen tickets. "Split Tickets by Course" cuts one chit per course. "Kitchen Names" renames "FIRE", "HELD", "CHANGED", "VOIDED" and course labels. "Flash Changes" flashes new, fired and changed tickets. "KDS Ticket Headers" makes either the check number or the table number large. — [Toast Kitchen settings reference](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminKitchenDiningRoomReference.html)
- **Toast map colors:** red means items held and not sent, yellow means items sent, and green means all items fulfilled, which requires a KDS. These are table-level states, not course-level. — [Toast: Create Service Areas](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430). In the API, `fulfillmentStatus` is `NEW`, `HOLD`, `SENT` or `READY`. — [Toast API: Selection](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/orders/tag/Data-definitions/schema/Selection/)
- **Toast history:** POS v2.89 fixed a bug so that tickets print when later courses fire under course pacing with item routing. Before the fix, only the first course printed, with the first course labeled "FIRE" and the rest "HOLD". — [Toast release announcement (snippet)](https://updates.toasttab.com/announcements/ann_T6Rin0AbsoUVl). A community manager said that with fire by course, "the KDS would separate each order by course rather than by ticket", which can confuse staff. — [Toast Community (community, undated; snippet)](https://community.toasttab.com/t5/back-office-team/how-do-you-group-items-on-the-kds/m-p/10747)
- **Square:** "Enable Course Management" is turned on per POS mode under Settings > Device Management > Modes. On the KDS, "Show fired and held courses" and "Only show fired courses" are the options, and a held course is not sent or printed until it is fired. A full ticket summary and "View all courses" show everything for a table. Hold and fire happen only in the POS app, not on the KDS. Square KDS runs only on Android. — [Square: Coursing with Square KDS](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/7748). The app listing promises "individual course lines", "one-tap firing and holding" and setting "any item to auto-fire". — [Square Restaurants POS, App Store (marketing)](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id1346862885). Older community answers said coursing "is not available with Square KDS". The current help article supersedes them. — [Square community (community, undated)](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Orders-Menu-Items-Catalog/Group-by-Course-or-Seat-in-Square-KDS/m-p/295622)
- **Lightspeed K:** a "Fire course" button sits on the Register screen and in order Actions. Firing "print[s] a ticket to the linked printer reminding them of the next course". The floor plan's "Table status" setting can show coursing status. — [Lightspeed K glossary](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050430754)
- **TouchBistro:** the "Send" icon sends all unsent items, and the "Course" icon sends drinks and apps before mains. Up to 12 courses are supported. A whole course or a single item can be held, which sends a hold ticket. "Holds are not supported if your venue is using the Kitchen Display System (KDS)." Firing prints a fire order. — [TouchBistro: Sending drinks and courses at separate times (snippet)](https://cdn.touchbistro.com/help/articles/sending-drinks-and-courses-at-separate-times)
- **Revel:** course firing paces appetizers, entrées and desserts, and "Course 0" is often used for appetizers. With "Optimize KDS for Coursing", items reach the KDS only when their course is ready. — [Revel: TSR Course Fire (snippet)](https://support.revelsystems.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015382951-TSR-Course-Fire-); [Revel: Optimize KDS for Coursing (snippet)](https://support.revelsystems.com/s/article/Optimize-KDS-for-Coursing-1582899129817)
- **Simphony:** enhanced tables show coursing overlays on the floor page. — [Simphony: Adding Enhanced Dining Tables](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/food-beverage/simphony/simcg/t_tms_front_of_house_enhanced_dining_tables.htm). TMS can "update table status based on coursing". — [Simphony: Enhanced Table Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/food-beverage/simphony/simcg/c_tms_enhanced.htm). Simphony also documents holding selected items for a preset time and suppressing dining courses on the KDS (titles only seen). — [Simphony: Holding items for a preset time](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/F32325_01/doc.192/f32329/t_checks_hold_selected_entire_preset.htm); [Simphony KDS: suppress dining courses](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/F32325_01/doc.192/f32331/t_kds_suppress_dining_courses.htm)
- **Lavu:** staff can send a specific course or all unsent items. "You cannot use course labels if you are using Hold & Fire." — [Lavu: Active Seat and Course](https://support.lavu.com/en/knowledge/pos-active-seat-and-course)
- **Aloha:** course numbers are assigned to items as part of "course ordering" (title only seen). — [Aloha POS: Assigning course numbers to items](https://docs.ncrvoyix.com/restaurant/aloha-pos/implementing/course_ordering/assigning_course_numbers_to_items)
- **Clover:** "Coursing" (pre-assigning items to courses) is a feature request marked "Deferred" with 8 votes, so Clover Dining apparently has no coursing. — [Clover UserVoice](https://clover.uservoice.com/forums/963884-restaurant/category/510038-clover-dining)
- **SpotOn:** a May 2026 update syncs "course progression and payments… to OpenTable in real-time" so hosts can seat the next party without walking the floor. — [SpotOn product updates, May 2026 (snippet)](https://updates.spoton.com/announcements/product-updates-weeks-05-01-26-05-31-26-2)

### Inferences
- Overcooked's "SET" signals and fired courses correspond to Toast's expediter-fired and sequential automated firing, where the next course fires once prep stations or expo mark the previous one done. Toast's "Previous Course Status" on tickets is the nearest analog to showing course progress in the kitchen.
- Several vendors have settings that cannot be combined: Toast's course pacing and server item firing, Lavu's course labels and Hold & Fire, and TouchBistro's holds and KDS. This suggests coursing was bolted on. A single coherent course-state model across POS, map and KDS is a chance for Overcooked to be simpler.
- The table map rarely shows which course a table is on. Toast's colors cover only held, sent and fulfilled. Course-aware table status is a feature of enterprise or advanced systems (Simphony overlays, Lightspeed K's coursing-status view). For a tasting-menu room, a per-course, per-table Service Board would be an advance over most products.

### Gaps
- No vendor document showed per-seat course progress on the map or KDS, for example seat 3 being on a different course.
- Toast's "Stay" action is named but not defined in the article.
- Square's auto-fire mechanics and Lightspeed K's KDS course display were not found.

## 4. Table transfers, merges/joins, splits, moves between areas, and history

### Takeaway
"Merging" usually means combining checks. Only Square, with linked tables, and Lightspeed K, with a chain icon, show a visible link between tables on the map, and Simphony merges last only as long as the active layout. Seat-level moves exist in Aloha (Transfer Seat), Square (Transfer to Check) and Toast (move an item to a seat after sending). Moving between areas works through the area navigation in Toast and Aloha. No vendor documents an audit trail. Kitchen notification on moves (Lightspeed) and on seat changes (Toast) is the only "history" shown.

### Cited Findings
- **Toast:** press and hold a table, select the check, choose "Move X Check", pick the destination (another service area via the navigation bar), then Confirm. If the destination already has a check, staff choose whether to merge into one check or keep separate orders. Moving needs permission "1.16 Change Table", and moving between servers needs "1.17 Change Server". "Only one server can be assigned per table", and a check stays attributed to the server who opened it. The article does not cover history or audit. — [Toast: Manage Tables With Toast POS (updated Sep 17, 2026)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/New-POS-Managing-Tables)
- **Square:** staff can merge tables for large parties, and merged tables "display as linked and occupied". Merging works on Square Register, while Square Terminal and Handheld can only view merged tables. — [Square: Building your floor plan](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6427); [Square: Manage seats](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8583-manage-seats-in-your-restaurant). "Transfer to Check" moves a seat to another check, where it becomes a new seat. "Create new check" splits one seat out. — [Square: Manage seats](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8583-manage-seats-in-your-restaurant)
- **Lightspeed K:** "Link to another table" works only if the second table is empty, and linked tables show a chain icon. "Transfer table" moves the entire order, and "the prep station that received the order is sent a ticket about the transfer". "Transfer user" moves the order to another staff member. — [Lightspeed K glossary](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050430754)
- **Aloha Cloud:** staff can move table to table, table to tab, tab to table, and a split check to a table or tab. Moving onto an occupied target requires owning both. For "Transfer Seat", staff long-press the seat number, then tap the target table in the same room or navigate to another room. Seats can also go to a tab. "Take ownership" moves financial responsibility and needs "Manager Approval" without permission. The article does not cover combining tables or audit trails. — [Aloha Cloud: Transferring tabs, tables, and seats](https://docs.ncrvoyix.com/restaurant/aloha-cloud/using/transferring_tabs_tables_seats_mp). Aloha's "Pivot seating transfer merges seats" setting controls whether same-numbered seats merge or are renumbered. — [Aloha Store Settings](https://docs.ncrvoyix.com/restaurant/aloha-pos/implementing/field_definitions/store_settings_order_entry_group)
- **Simphony:** "Merge Table" joins two or more tables "until the layout is deactivated or changed", and parent and child tables both update their decorator. "Pickup Table (Check#)" transfers check ownership. "Unseat Table" returns the guest to the wait list, and if a check was on the table, "it becomes lost". "Select Employee does not move pre-existing checks." — [Simphony: Enhanced Table Status and Functions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/food-beverage/simphony/sipou/c_tms_table_status.htm). When merging, TMS can prompt for a seat count that defaults to the merged tables' combined seats. — [Simphony 19.x: Dining Room Tables and Seating (snippet)](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/food-beverage/simphony/19.4/simcg/c_tms_chapter_heading.htm)
- **TouchBistro:** reviewers cite "easy table transfers if diners want to change tables". — [NerdWallet / FitSmallBusiness TouchBistro reviews (third-party, snippet)](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/small-business/touchbistro-review). A "Combining seats" help article exists, but its content was unavailable. — [TouchBistro](https://cdn.touchbistro.com/help/articles/combining-seats)
- **Clover:** "Merging Tables for Large Groups" (A1 + A2 = A1A2) has 63 votes and is "Started". "Transfer Server on Closed Order" is "Under Review". — [Clover UserVoice](https://clover.uservoice.com/forums/963884-restaurant/category/510038-clover-dining)
- **SpotOn:** a third-party review says staff can "move or merge tables, and reassign checks mid-service". — [Expert Market SpotOn review (third-party, snippet)](https://www.expertmarket.com/pos/spoton-review)
- **Tevalis:** the brochure lists "transfer tables" and "merge tables". — [Tevalis brochure (marketing)](https://issuu.com/marketingattevalis/docs/tevalis_epos_brochure_)

### Inferences
- Because Overcooked's tables are restaurant-wide and seat numbers are the same in every layout, a merge could keep each original table's seat identity, for example T4-S1 and T5-S1, instead of Aloha-style renumbering or merging. It could then show the join on the active layout only, as Simphony scopes merges to the layout.
- A kitchen notice on moves (Lightspeed's transfer ticket) and on seat changes (Toast's change notice) is how existing systems keep the kitchen's view correct. Overcooked's per-table tickets with seat restrictions need the same: if a guest changes seat, their restrictions must follow on the ticket.
- Ownership and permission controls are standard: Toast's permissions 1.16 and 1.17, Aloha's ownership with manager approval, and Simphony's pickup. Simphony's "check becomes lost" on unseat is a warning case for data safety.

### Gaps
- No vendor documented an audit or history view for table moves, merges, seat moves or splits.
- Toast does not document a physical table join on the map, as opposed to a check merge.
- Square's seat numbering after a table merge is undocumented.

## 6. Offline behavior and multiple devices editing or using the same floor plan

### Takeaway
Two offline designs dominate. On-premises servers keep running when the internet drops: Aloha, Simphony with an on-site posting service, and TouchBistro with an on-site Mac. Cloud POSs instead fall back to a local relay. Toast uses a single wired "local hub", which keeps POS-to-KDS flowing but limits order sharing between POS devices. Toast tells staff that "each employee choose a single device". No vendor documents how conflicts are resolved when several devices edit the same check or floor plan. Floor-plan edits go through a central publish or reload step.

### Cited Findings
- **Toast offline mode with local sync:** one hub per restaurant, assigned automatically. It must be hardwired Ethernet, not a handheld or Elo V1, on the restaurant network, and must have pinged within the last 3 minutes. "The local hub device relays updates from one device to other devices and to the Toast platform." "KDS devices can receive orders from POS devices on the same local network." Not available offline: "sharing non-KDS orders between devices", kiosks, logging in or out, EMV (cards must be swiped), gift cards and loyalty, and guest lookup. Separate subnets cannot share data. "Toast recommends each employee choose a single device to place and update orders." Conflict handling is not described. — [Toast platform guide: Offline mode with local sync](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformOfflineModeLocalSync.html)
- **Toast's older standard offline mode:** devices lose contact with each other, and orders added on one device "do not appear on other devices". — [Toast platform guide: Offline mode (snippet)](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformOfflineMode.html). Toast "plans to deprecate the offline mode method and switch entirely to offline mode with local sync". If courses are fired from the expediter KDS and every ticket prints to prep stations once fired, "these course tickets do not print during offline mode with local sync". — [Toast: Use Toast in Offline Mode (updated Mar 25, 2026; snippet)](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Using-Toast-in-Offline-Mode). The expediter "Printing Mode" includes "Only print expediter tickets when Toast is in offline mode". — [Toast Kitchen settings](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminKitchenDiningRoomReference.html)
- **Toast floor edits** happen only in Toast Web and reach devices after "Save, then publish". If they do not appear, staff "resync all data". — [Toast: Create Service Areas](https://support.toasttab.com/en/article/Creating-Service-Areas-and-Table-Setup-1493049150430). In local-sync offline mode, "Resync Orders and Resync ALL Data" are unavailable. — [Toast: Offline mode with local sync](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformOfflineModeLocalSync.html)
- **Lightspeed K** floor plan changes reach each POS only after a manual "Reload" in Settings > Control center. — [Lightspeed K: Editing floor plans](https://k-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/articles/1260804656729)
- **TouchBistro:** "offline mode allows you to continue taking/closing orders, sending orders to the KDS, and taking offline payments". — [TouchBistro site (marketing, snippet)](https://www.touchbistro.com/?p=37760). Reservations become "read only" while offline if the user stays logged in. — [TouchBistro: Reservations iPad App – Off Line (snippet)](https://cdn.touchbistro.com/help/articles/reservations-ipad-app-off-line)
- **Cross-vendor summary (competitor blog, Katalyst, June 10, 2026):** TouchBistro runs multi-iPad sites off an on-site Mac server, but card processing, cloud reporting and emailed receipts need internet. SpotOn connects stations, handhelds and kitchen screens over the local network, ships a 4G backup router, and online orders stop reaching the kitchen during an outage. On Aloha and Simphony the full database is on site, Simphony has an on-premise posting service, and workstations are "largely unaffected". Lightspeed is cloud with a local cache, and some product lines have an optional local server. Toast enters offline mode about 40 seconds after losing connectivity. Square made offline payments the default as of April 2026. Clover stores offline payments for up to 7 days. — [Katalyst blog: What happens to your POS when the internet goes down (competitor blog)](https://www.katalystos.com/blog/what-happens-to-your-pos-when-the-internet-goes-down)
- **Aloha Cloud:** floor plan changes made on a POS are saved with "Apply Changes". The article does not say how they reach other terminals. — [Aloha Cloud: Creating a floor plan](https://docs.ncrvoyix.com/restaurant/aloha-cloud/using/creating_a_floor_plan)
- **Network caveat (Toast):** don't power-cycle the router while offline, use separate modem and router devices, and consider a cellular backup. — [Toast: Offline mode with local sync](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformOfflineModeLocalSync.html)

### Inferences
- The market's de facto rule for multiple devices offline is one writer per check or employee: Toast's "single device" guidance, plus ownership rules in Aloha and Simphony. Overcooked runs offline-first on several tablets, where several people may touch one table's seats (the sommelier on drinks, a captain on courses). It therefore needs conflict rules the incumbents never document. Options include ownership per field or per seat, operation-based merges, and an append-only course and SET event log.
- Floor-plan editing everywhere is a central, publish-gated action, not concurrent live editing. Overcooked can reasonably lock layout editing during service, or allow only draft edits with explicit publish, rather than solving concurrent geometry merges.
- Toast's hub requirement (wired, single, same subnet) and its "course tickets do not print" offline edge case show that local relays have fragile spots around expo-driven firing. This is exactly where Overcooked's SET and fired-course signals sit.

### Gaps
- No vendor documented what happens when two devices edit the same check or the same floor plan at once, whether online or offline.
- Toast's doc is ambiguous on sharing between POS devices offline. It says the hub relays updates but also that non-KDS orders are not shared.
- Device-to-device sync during offline mode was not documented for Square, Clover or Lightspeed K.

### Inferences
- The biggest friction in seat-level service is the number of taps needed to attach an item to a seat during service. Overcooked pre-builds each seat's menu, language, restrictions and pairings before service, which largely removes that friction. It is worth stating explicitly when positioning the product.
- Demand for seat-level guest identity is clear: it is Clover's top request with 112 votes. That supports Overcooked's per-seat profiles (language, restrictions) and suggests showing a guest name or initials at the seat position on the map.
- Recurring asks map directly to Overcooked features: course status on the map, time on table, and re-fire without voiding. These should be first-class features rather than add-ons.
